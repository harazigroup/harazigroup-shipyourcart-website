Malaysia is not only about travel. Many of its products are now in demand across the GCC for their quality and prices, and most are halal. These are the things our customers ask about most, with a tip for each.

1. Malaysian white coffee

White coffee from Ipoh is known for its smooth taste. The best-known brand is OLDTOWN, with flavours like Classic, Hazelnut, Salted Caramel and Less Sugar. It is light, easy to ship and makes a nice gift.

OLDTOWN flavours are in our shop, order directly and we ship them to you.

2. Modest fashion and hijabs

Malaysia is one of the world's biggest modest fashion markets: tudung (hijabs) in many fabrics and colours, abayas, jubahs and baju kurung. Local brands launch new collections every season, especially before Ramadan and Eid.

Tip: clothes are light, so combine several orders in one box to get the most from each kilo.

3. Cosmetic raw materials

If you make serums, creams or soap at home, you need raw materials like hyaluronic acid, niacinamide, vitamin C and kojic acid. They are available in Malaysia for less than many stores charge.

See raw materials in our shop, order from a single item.

4. Malaysian skincare and haircare

Several Malaysian skincare and haircare brands are trending on TikTok, many of them halal and made for hot weather. Order from the brand's official store on Shopee or Lazada to your Malaysian address.

5. Spices, sauces and snacks

Malaysian curry pastes, sambal and local snacks are popular, especially with people who visited Malaysia and loved the food.

Tip: every country has its own rules on imported food. Choose factory-sealed products with a long expiry date, and ask us before ordering large amounts.

6. Batik and traditional gifts

Hand-drawn batik fabric and wooden or brass crafts make special gifts. Watch the size: big light items are charged by volumetric weight (read about it here).

7. Wholesale products for your store

If you run a store in the GCC and want to sell Malaysian products, we can source them in bulk from the factory or distributor and ship them. See Product sourcing.
