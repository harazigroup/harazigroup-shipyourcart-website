Found something in a Malaysian store, but the seller doesn't ship to the GCC, rejects your card, or asks for a Malaysian phone number? That's what our Buy for Me service is for: send us the link, and we buy, receive, photograph and ship it to your door.

When do you need "Buy for Me"?

  • The store rejects foreign cards or only takes local payments like DuitNow and FPX.
  • Sign-up needs a Malaysian phone number for the verification code.
  • The seller doesn't reply or doesn't understand your request in English.
  • You want items from several stores and don't have time to follow up each order.
  • Local shops and brands that have no online store at all.

How it works: 4 steps

  1. Send the links on WhatsApp: each product link with colour, size and quantity, plus your country and city.
  2. Get a full quotation: product prices as listed in the store, the service fee and estimated shipping to your country. Everything is written down before you pay.
  3. Pay and we buy: pay by card (Visa or Mastercard) or USDT. We order to our warehouse address and send you the purchase invoice.
  4. We receive, photograph and ship: when the orders arrive we send you photos, consolidate them into one box, and share the final weight and tracking number.

Two examples from real orders

1. Three stores, one box

A customer in Dubai ordered from 3 different Shopee sellers: cotton trousers, a games-console part and a TV mainboard. Shipped separately, that would be 3 parcels, each paying the expensive "first kilo". We consolidate them into one box of about 4 kg, so he pays for a single shipment.

Tips to pay less

  • Order everything at once: the service fee and the first kilo are shared across all items, so each item costs less.
  • Watch out for bulky items: we vacuum-pack pillows, duvets and large soft toys.
  • Choose sellers inside Malaysia: items marked "Overseas" can take weeks to reach us.
  • Electronics: boards and parts without batteries ship normally, but loose batteries are not allowed. See the prohibited items list.

Quick questions

Can you buy from any store? Any store or shop in Malaysia, online or physical, as long as the item is allowed for shipping.

Will I pay customs in my country? Any duty or VAT is assessed by your country's customs on arrival and depends on the order's value and type. We don't add it to our invoice.

What if something is wrong with the item? We photograph it as soon as it arrives, so if the colour or size is wrong we contact the seller before shipping. Returns follow the store's own policy.
