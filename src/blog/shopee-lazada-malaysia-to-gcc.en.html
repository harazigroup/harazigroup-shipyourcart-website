Shopee and Lazada have thousands of Malaysian products at great prices, but most sellers do not ship to the GCC. The fix is simple: order to your own Malaysian address, and we receive and ship it to you. Here are the full steps.

1. Open a free account and get your address

Sign up at Ship Your Cart in a minute. Your account shows:

  • Your customer code, like SYC-10001. It tells us which parcels are yours.
  • Your Malaysian address (our warehouse in Selangor), with a copy button.

The account and address are free. You only pay when you ship.

2. Add the address on Shopee or Lazada

In the app, add a new address and copy the details from your account exactly. Most important:

Put your code next to your name, for example: Ahmed SYC-10001. Without the code we cannot tell the parcel is yours.
  • Phone: use the number shown in your address, so the courier can reach us.
  • State, city and postcode: copy them exactly, the app uses them for delivery.

3. Pick the right seller

  • Choose sellers inside Malaysia. Items marked "Overseas" or shipped from China can take weeks to reach the warehouse.
  • Check ratings and sales before you order.
  • Make sure the item is not on the prohibited items list, such as loose batteries or flammable liquids.

4. Paying

Shopee and Lazada usually accept Visa and Mastercard, but sometimes a foreign card is declined or sign-up needs a Malaysian number. If that happens, send us the link and we buy it for you with Buy for me. You pay us by card or USDT.

5. We receive, photograph and combine

When your order reaches the warehouse, we add it to your account with photos and weight. Let your orders collect, then select the parcels and request shipping. The more you put in one box, the more you save.

6. Pay for shipping and track it

You see the price in your account, choose the carrier and pay by card or USDT. Then track it with the tracking number all the way to your door.

New customer? Get 10% off your first shipment (up to RM 50).

Quick questions

How long from Malaysia to the GCC? Air shipping usually arrives within a few working days after we ship. See prices on the pricing page and ask us about your country.

How long can parcels stay in the warehouse? We keep them while you finish shopping. Storage details are confirmed when you register.

Can I order from stores other than Shopee and Lazada? Yes, any Malaysian online store that delivers inside Malaysia.
