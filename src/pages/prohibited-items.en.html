Before you buy

Prohibited and restricted items

Airlines, carriers and customs in every country have rules. Check this list before you order, and ask us if you are not sure.

Ask before you buy

We cannot ship

Explosives, fireworks, ammunition and weapons, including replicas and parts
Flammable liquids and gases, fuel, lighters and gas cylinders
Poisons, corrosives, acids and other hazardous chemicals
Illegal drugs and controlled substances
Cash, cheques, bank cards and other money instruments
Counterfeit or pirated goods
Live animals, plants and seeds
Fresh, frozen or other perishable food
Pornographic or offensive material
Anything banned by Malaysian law or the law of the destination country

Ask us first

Lithium batteries, power banks and devices with batteries
Perfumes, nail polish and other alcohol-based products
Aerosols and sprays
Medicines, vitamins and supplements
Cosmetics and skin care in large quantities
Packaged food and drinks
Liquids, gels and powders
Magnets, knives and tools
Branded goods in commercial quantities

Rules change by country and carrier

DHL, Aramex and FedEx each have their own limits, and each destination has its own import rules. Some items can be shipped with one carrier but not another. Message us the product link and we will check it for you.

If a prohibited item arrives

We will tell you and keep it aside. We can return it to the seller in Malaysia at your cost, or dispose of it if it cannot legally be kept. We cannot ship it.

You are responsible for what you buy

When you request a shipment you confirm the contents and their value are correct. Wrong or missing information can delay your shipment or lead to fines at the destination.
