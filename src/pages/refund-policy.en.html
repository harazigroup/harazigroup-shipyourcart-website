Cancellations and refunds

Simple rules, based on where your shipment is.

Ask about a refund

You get a full refund

You cancel a request before you pay: nothing to pay
You paid, but the shipment has not been handed to the carrier yet
We cannot ship your items for a reason on our side
You were charged twice for the same shipment

Not refundable

Shipping charges after the shipment is handed to the carrier
Customs duties, taxes and fines charged by the destination country
Charges caused by wrong recipient details or refused delivery
Items held or returned by customs because they are prohibited or wrongly declared

How refunds are paid

Card payments are refunded to the same card through Stripe. Banks usually take 5 to 10 working days to show it. USDT payments are refunded in USDT to the wallet you paid from.

Returning items to the seller

If you want to return something to a Malaysian store before it ships, tell us. We send it back to the seller, and you pay the local postage. Refunds for the item itself come from the store, not from us.

Purchases made for you

For our Buy for me service, refunds for the product follow the store's own return policy. Our service fee is not refundable once the order is placed.
