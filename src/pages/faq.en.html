Which countries do you ship to?

We ship from Malaysia to most countries, including the Gulf, and we also import into Malaysia. Ask us about your destination.

How is the shipping price calculated?

By weight or size (whichever is greater), destination and shipping method. We always confirm the price first.

Can you combine orders from different stores?

Yes. That is what Shop and Ship is for — we combine your orders into one shipment.

Who pays customs duties?

Duties and taxes are set by the destination country and are usually paid by the receiver. We'll explain the options for your shipment.

Are there items you cannot ship?

Yes — dangerous goods, illegal items and some restricted products. Ask us before you buy.

How do I pay?
