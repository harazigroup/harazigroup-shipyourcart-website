Shipping policy

How shipping works with us

Clear rules, so you know exactly what you pay and what to expect.

Questions? WhatsApp us

Weight and price

We charge the chargeable weight: the actual weight or the volumetric weight (L × W × H in cm ÷ 5000), whichever is higher
Carriers round weight up, usually to the next 0.5 kg
Prices are door to door and shown in Malaysian ringgit (RM)
Your price is confirmed before you pay, and you pay before we ship
New customers get a discount on their first shipment, shown automatically

Customs and delivery

Import duties and taxes are set by the destination country and are usually paid by the receiver
You must declare the true contents and value of every shipment
Delivery times are the carrier's estimates and are not guaranteed
You get a tracking number as soon as your shipment leaves Malaysia
Customs in the destination may inspect, hold or return a shipment

Receiving your orders

Use your Malaysian address and customer code on every order. We record each parcel, photograph it and show it in your account. Parcels without your code may take longer to identify.

Packing and consolidation

We combine your parcels into one shipment and pack them for international transport. We may remove extra boxes to reduce weight, unless you ask us to keep the original packaging.

Damage or loss

Check your shipment when it arrives and contact us right away if anything is damaged or missing, with photos. We will open a claim with the carrier. Compensation follows the carrier's liability limits.

Wrong address or refused delivery

Please check the recipient details before you pay. Extra charges for address changes, failed delivery or returns are paid by the customer.
