Questions

Amazon FBA prep FAQ

Which Amazon marketplaces do you ship to?

Any Amazon FBA warehouse: Saudi Arabia, UAE, US, UK, Europe, Australia, Singapore and more. Tell us the marketplace and we’ll quote.

Who creates the shipment in Seller Central?

You create the shipment plan in your Seller Central account and send us the FNSKU and box labels. If it’s your first time, we’ll guide you.

Who handles customs at the destination?

Amazon does not act as the importer, so each shipment needs customs clearance. We’ll explain the options for your marketplace, including door-to-door delivery with duties paid where available.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of units, the prep each product needs, and the destination. Send us the details on WhatsApp and we’ll send you a clear quote.
